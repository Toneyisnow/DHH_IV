City daylight redesign — 2026-10-15

Reference: Screenshots/city_sample.mp4, sampled throughout its 51-second duration.
The reference informed perspective, camera following, layered depth, water edges,
and sprite characters. The new art is original Blender geometry, set in daylight.

Assets
- ../ArtSource/CityDaylight/DaylightKit.blend: reusable collections. Enable one collection
  at a time to inspect it at the origin; collections are hidden for sprite rendering.
- Tools/build_city_daylight.py: deterministic Blender 3.5 generator. Builds models first,
  saves the .blend, then renders eight directions and eight gait frames per direction.
- Tools/pack_city_walker.py: packs registered RGBA frames into a 1024 x 1280 atlas.
- Assets/Resources/CityDaylight: runtime mesh JSON and the character atlas.
- Market A/B, town hall, shipyard, tavern, house, olive tree, stone bridge share a
  plaster/timber/slate palette. Runtime model parts are batched by material.

Scene
- 108 x 72 world-unit city footprint, plus shipyard deck and surrounding water.
- Perspective camera: 32-degree pitch, 42-degree vertical FOV, smooth following;
  moving deeper into town shortens the camera distance from 31 toward 26 units.
  The city spans approximately three view-widths across and two view-widths deep; perspective varies
  ground coverage with depth.
- WASD / arrow keys and click-to-walk. Expanded half-unit walk grid samples real
  bridge/stair surfaces. Building footprints and canal gaps block navigation.
- Two markets lead to the existing market interface. Tavern leads to companions.
  Town hall, shipyard, industry and harbor retain their existing workflows.
- All eight city definitions use the new kit. Venice has a longitudinal canal;
  the other ports use the terraced market and transverse waterfront canal layout.
- Sun and local lanterns cast real shadows. Cutout sprite shaders cast and receive
  shadows. Depth-aware disk blur keeps the player in focus and softens near/far scenery.

Pixel-painted material revision
- Original AI-generated diffuse atlas: Assets/Resources/CityDaylight/Textures/PixelMaterials.png.
  Six cells: cobblestone, aged plaster, slate, walnut timber, olive leaves, cut masonry.
  Reference screenshots informed palette/pixel painting; no screenshot pixels were extracted.
- CitySurface projects the atlas in world space with point filtering, fixed pixel grids,
  inset cell samples and dominant-axis blending. Leaf saturation is restrained; canvas
  uses discrete woven/fold shading. The scene retains real directional and local shadows.
- Cooler, darker ambient light and stronger sun shadows increase daylight contrast.
- Added material identifiers to mesh JSON and the Blender exporter. Vertices and triangle
  indices in all eight models remain byte-equivalent after JSON normalization versus HEAD.
  GeometryHashes.json records the normalized geometry fingerprints.
- Map, camera-follow movement, player atlas and UI layout retained.
